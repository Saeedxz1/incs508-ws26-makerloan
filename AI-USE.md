## 4. What the AI generated and what people must own
- **Generated by AI:** the text of the SRS, all prototype source code, the demo script, the README files and the first draft of this log.
- **Values proposed by the AI that must be confirmed with the client:** loan periods, reservation window and hold time, notification times, 14-day presumed-lost limit, 12-month briefing validity, and which categories need a briefing (SRS 2.7, 5.5 and Appendix C).
- **GitHub publication:** Claude created the private repository `Saeedxz1/incs508-ws26-makerloan`, the `Itr1` branch and the first commits through the browser, signed in as Saeedxz1 (Saeed Mahmoud), at the team's request. Commits by the other members are made by those members with their own GitHub accounts.
- **Seed data:** names, IDs, items and loan history in the prototype are invented for the demonstration. The loan history comes from a seeded pseudo-random generator.
